Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Privacy Overview

At Online Timetable Creator (accessible at timetablecreator.online), your privacy is one of our utmost concerns. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of data we record, how we process and protect your schedules, and how we handle database integrations securely.

2. How We Collect and Use Your Data

We process data differently based on how you interact with our schedule planner:

  • Offline Storage: When you build timetables locally, your classes, timings, and schedules are stored entirely inside your browser's local state (IndexedDB/localStorage) and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Cloud Database Synchronization: If you choose to save or share your routine, your timetable configuration is encrypted and stored inside a secure Firestore database so that you can reload it using your custom workspace links.
  • AI Assistant Input: When using our AI scheduler copilot, your descriptive prompts are transmitted securely to Google Gemini AI models to generate structured schedule JSON configurations. No personal identifying files are attached to these requests.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We do not employ invasive marketing trackers, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting tools. We may use highly minimalist operational cookies or browser state flags purely to persist your UI theme preferences, maintain local workspace sessions, and analyze core performance.

4. Security Specifications

All user interactions, API endpoints, database operations, and exports are conducted over HTTPS connections encrypted via industry-standard 256-bit TLS/SSL certificates. We implement strict cloud boundaries and Firestore rules to prevent unauthorized users from retrieving or modifying saved agendas.

5. Data Erasure Rights

You maintain absolute control over your rosters. If you want to delete a shared timetable from our cloud database, you can do so directly using the clear/reset commands inside your interactive workspace or by dropping our support desk an email.
